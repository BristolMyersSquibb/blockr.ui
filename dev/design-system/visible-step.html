The visible step: what changes on screen

Your decision was three text levels: default (near-black), muted (mid grey) and disabled (light grey, for disabled controls only). Two older names don't fit that and still hold their old colours. The "visible step" is switching them. Everything below uses blockr.ui's real stylesheets; the columns differ only in those two colours.

Change 1 is not about contrast: grey-700 already passes easily. It is about having three text levels instead of five. It removes a colour that sits between "main text" and "muted" and is hard to tell apart from main text (the two are only 1.7:1 from each other). Change 2 is about contrast: light grey text is hard to read and fails the accessibility minimum.

What the step is, mechanically

Two lines in blockr-tokens.css: --blockr-color-text-secondary points at text-default (or text-muted for the alternative), and --blockr-color-text-subtle points at text-muted. Every package that uses those names follows at once: 74 places use "secondary" and 101 use "subtle", across 13 packages. A few spots read the grey directly instead of the name, like the input placeholder here, and need a one-line edit each.
