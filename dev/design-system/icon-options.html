Small icons: five ways

Drag handles, remove buttons and the fold arrow on a column list. Mid grey (B) was too heavy for you; light grey (A) is quiet but only 2.5:1, below the 3:1 WCAG asks of anything you click or drag. Colour is one lever; the shape's weight and when an icon is shown are the other two. Hover the rows, or tab into them.

Proposal: E. At rest the card shows only its content and the fold arrow, which is quieter than today. The handle and the remove button appear the moment you point at or tab to a row, in a colour that is clearly readable when you need it. It also matches the rule blockr's design system already has for remove buttons: hidden at rest, shown on hover, red when you are about to use it. D is the runner-up if you want the icons always visible.
