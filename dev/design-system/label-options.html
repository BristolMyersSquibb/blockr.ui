Labels: some options

The same block card six times, with blockr.ui's real stylesheets. Only the labels ("Dataset", "Number of rows", "X-axis") change; the values in the fields stay near-black in all of them, and the placeholder is mid grey everywhere. Colour is one way to make labels quieter; weight and letter case are the others, and they can be combined.

What this means for the design system. B and D use one of the three text colours you decided on (default or muted), so nothing new is needed.
