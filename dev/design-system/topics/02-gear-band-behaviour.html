Topic 2: the gear band, where it opens and how it closes

Your notes on G3 and G5. The floating panel reads as "something is open now", separate from the block, and the crossfilter felt better once its settings floated. But floating hides the chart you are changing, and an in-flow band that simply appears gives no sense that it pushed the block down. On a chart with many settings the chart ends up far below. The grey tray (T1) is decided and used in every option. Click the gear in each block to open and close it.

Q1Where the band opens

Proposal: B, in flow, sliding open. The quarter-second slide shows the chart being pushed down, which is the cue the instant band lacks, and the grey tray plus the beak give it the look of a separate, open thing, which is what you liked about the floating panel. Nothing is covered, so every change shows in the chart at once. The crossfilter's settings move into the same tray. Runner-up: D, if the chart dropping out of view is the bigger problem: on a block wider than about 700px the tray opens beside the chart instead of above it, and below that width it behaves like B.

Q2How it closes

Today six packages close the band only from the gear; three close it on any click outside. In every option the Escape key closes it too, and the open state lasts for the session (a re-render does not close it) but is not saved with the board.

Proposal: A, the gear and Escape only. The band is where you work while watching the output, so a click on the chart or on a menu inside the band must not close it; the gear is right above the tray and already shows the open state. Runner-up: B, a quiet × in the tray's corner, if a second way out near the fields turns out to be missed.

Recorded as they are

  • The tray is the grey tray from T1: subtle background, white fields, beak towards the gear, no heading (G4).
  • Changes apply as they are made; there is no Apply button in the band.
  • Several blocks can have their tray open at the same time.
