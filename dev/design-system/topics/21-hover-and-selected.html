Topic 21: hover and selected look the same

In light, bg-hover (a 6% wash of the text colour) lands at #f0f1f2 and bg-selected is grey-100, #f3f4f6. A hovered row or tab looks like the selected one. Dark does not have the problem: there, selected is a 10% wash and sits a step above hover.

Where bg-selected is used: the front dock tab and a selected row in a list (outline, views list, a table row). Menus are not affected: their current pick is weight 600 with no fill, and selected segments, pressed tools and the open gear use the accent tint. In each drawing below, "Filter rows" is selected and "Summarise" is held in hover; hover the others too.
