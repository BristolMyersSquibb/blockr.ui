Topic 1: telling the gear band apart from the block

Your point: the open gear band looks like the rest of the block. It is as wide as the block, white, with the same fields, so on a transform block you cannot tell the settings from the block's own controls. Five ways to set it apart, each in a transform block (pivot longer, fields on its face) and an output block (chart). Everything else stays as decided: in flow, under the header row, opened and closed by the gear.

Proposal: B, a grey tray. The band takes the subtle background and its fields turn white, so the settings read as a separate layer at a glance, with no extra line, heading or colour. It is also the one option that makes the fields inside look different from the fields on the face, which is the confusion you describe. In dark mode the tray takes the page colour, one step below the block. Runner-up: E, if the band should also point back at the gear by colour.
