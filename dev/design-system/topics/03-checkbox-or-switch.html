Topic 3: checkbox or switch

Your note on C1: next to inputs the switch looked better than a checkbox in its grey box, and the switch is nicer but maybe a little pretentious. Since then the grey box is gone (topic 6b: a bare checkbox, no shell), so the comparison here is bare against bare. One argument that did not come up: in blockr every setting takes effect the moment you change it, with no Apply button, and that is the job common design systems give the switch (a checkbox is ticked in a form and sent later). Each option in three places: the gear tray, a transform block's face, and the board options. Click them.

Q1The control for on/off

Proposal: B, a switch everywhere, label after it. A setting in blockr applies at once, and a switch says exactly that; next to 42px inputs it reads as a control where a 16px box reads as a form field. Label after the switch keeps it the same shape as the checkbox it replaces, so the grid, the rows and a lone "Drop rows with NA" do not change layout. One control means Blockr.checkbox becomes Blockr.switch and Shiny's checkboxInput is drawn as a switch by the same stylesheet. Runner-up: A, if the switch still feels too much once it is everywhere.
