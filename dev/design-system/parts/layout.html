Inside a block: anatomy, titles, grid

Builds on: tokens, text levels, labels, buttons. The other parts assume these proposals.

1Anatomy

Two shapes, both already built. An output block (chart, table, composer) puts its title and subtitle in the header row, beside the tools (blockr.viz since f81c21e), and keeps every setting in the gear; a setting reaches the face only as a slot word in the subtitle (decided 2026-09-09, the on-block mapping band was retired then). A transform block (dplyr) keeps its main fields on the face. This part records what exists; nothing in it is new.

The block's own title (the dock header above all this, 20px) is the largest heading. Inside a block there is no 16px "section header": the dock's .blockr-section-header is used nowhere inside a block today.

Proposal: the params grid is the one grid for fields, on the block's face and in the gear band alike: equal tracks, a 12px gap, columns stepping on the panel's width, clamped to the number of fields, with two ladders, "wide" for selects and text and "knobs" for numbers and flags. The flex-wrap and auto-fill layouts in the gear band go. A checkbox sits in the grid with an empty label row so it lines up with its neighbours. Spacing: 12px between columns, 16px between rows of fields, 24px before a new section title. Rows stay for lists you add to and reorder; they are a list, not fields. The params grid moves from blockr.extra to blockr.ui (already planned, because viz cannot import blockr.extra).

4Recorded as they are

  • The order inside a block: header row (with the output title for output blocks), gear band, controls on the face (transform blocks), body, caption, status line.
  • Header row: 26px tools, gear last, right-aligned; no gear when there is nothing to configure.
  • 4px from label to control; 42px controls.
  • Breakpoints follow the panel, never the viewport (container queries on the block).
