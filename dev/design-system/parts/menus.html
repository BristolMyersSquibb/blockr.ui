3Recorded as they are

  • An option shows its value first and its label after it in meta text; the label is cut first when space runs out; the full text is in the title.
  • Multi pickers list only what is not picked; the picks sit as tags in the control, or in a menu's head above the filter box.
  • A Select.menu names the role it sets in its title, because the word that opened it names only the value.
  • Long lists render up to 200 options and end with a count row that asks you to type; server-searched lists say how many values there are.
  • Empty and loading rows: centred, italic, muted ("No matches", "All selected", "Loading…").
  • The gear band and the crossfilter's search band sit in flow under the header and are not raised; their look is the gear part's.
  • Adding a block happens in the side panel, not a dialog.
